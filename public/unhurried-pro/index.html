Questions

Good to know

Do I need the free Unhurried theme?

Yes. Pro is a child theme, so it runs on top of Unhurried. Install the free theme first, then upload Pro.

Do I need WooCommerce?

Only for the shop. Without WooCommerce, Pro still adds the Lead story pattern and the shop pages stay out of the way.

Will I lose my content when I switch to Pro?

No. Pages, posts and Site Editor changes carry over, because Pro builds on the theme you already use.

What if it is not right for me?

Write to hello@switchcasestudio.com within 14 days of buying, with your Stripe receipt number, and we refund the full $49. No questions, no forms. Refunds go back to the original card through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Paste the licence key from your download page into Appearance, Unhurried Pro, and new versions show up in WordPress like any other theme update.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. Switch Case Studio can install Unhurried, add your content and photos, and tune the colours to your brand.
