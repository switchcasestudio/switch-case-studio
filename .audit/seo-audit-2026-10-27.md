# SEO audit: switchcasestudio.com, 2026-10-27

Delta audit after the 2026-10-09 audit (fixes 1-14 live). First audit with real Google data: Search Console, GA4 and PageSpeed are connected through the `claude-seo` plugin (project `claude-seo-513000`, read-only service account).

## Verdict

On-page SEO is clean. The problems are now off-page: Google has not indexed 18 of 61 pages, the new service pages are slow on phones, and the brand name is not yet an entity Google recognizes.

## Data (last 28 days, Search Console)

- 449 impressions, 9 clicks, CTR 2.0%, average position 10.7.
- Nearly all impressions are Portland web-design queries. Position 1 for "portland web designer", "web designer portland", "local web designer", "portland web designers"; 4.3 "website designer"; 8 "website design portland or"; 11.6 "web designer near me".
- The Google Business Profile link (`/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`) is reported as its own page, so home-page queries split between two rows.
- GA4 organic: 2 sessions in 28 days (consented visitors only; undercounts).
- CrUX: no field data, the origin is below Chrome's traffic threshold. Speed below is lab data only.

## Findings, ranked

1. **18 of 61 sitemap pages are not indexed.** 13 "Discovered, currently not indexed", 5 "URL is unknown to Google": `/projects`, `/shop`, `/accessibility`, `/pricing/ai-automation`, five posts (about page, onboarding, website cost guide, fonts, office hours) and ten case studies (renewed-bodyworks, florida-green-improvements, zahav-medspa, scout, jo-marketing-11, crimson-equities, sha-design-studio, birth-of-venus, florida-energy-assistance). Not a links problem: `/projects` has 19 in-content links, the website cost guide 13. The sitemap was read 2026-10-27 with 61 URLs and no errors. Cause: Google crawls a young, low-authority site slowly. Fix: request indexing for the priority pages in Search Console (about 10 a day), then earn outside links (finding 3). The Indexing API is for job and livestream pages only; do not use it.
2. **The four service pages are the slowest pages on phones.** Lighthouse mobile performance is 62-65 with LCP 6.8-7.9s (brand page 7.9s), against 73 and 4.3s on the home page and 85 and 3.5s on a blog post. CLS is 0-0.006 and TBT under 200ms everywhere, so the cost is load, not shifting or blocking. The top Lighthouse opportunities are unused JavaScript and CSS. The pricing board for web development is 67 with LCP 5.8s.
3. **The brand is not an entity yet.** A search for "Switch Case Studio" surfaces nothing of ours; `switchcasestudios.com` is "SwitchCase Studios", a software and product engineering firm, and a "SwitchCase" agency exists in Sydney. The Organization schema has the right name and Portland address and 7 `sameAs` profiles, but no Bluesky (owner's, bio still says Phoenix), no Wikidata and no Clutch.
4. **`/services` is recorded as "Page with redirect" to `/pricing`,** from a crawl on 2026-10-10 before `/services` came back as its own index page. It returns 200 now and needs a recrawl.
5. **Low click-through.** The site already ranks in the top 10 for its local terms, and 449 impressions gave 9 clicks. The titles and descriptions of `/` and the service pages are the lever.

## Checked and clean

- All 61 sitemap pages: 200, title at most 60, description at most 160, self-canonical, one h1 inside `<main>`, JSON-LD parses, no hidden-body SSG pages. Exception: `/unhurried-pro/` has no JSON-LD.
- All 16 named redirects in `netlify.toml` reach a 200 in one hop. `http://www` takes two hops (`http://www` → `https://www` → apex); harmless.
- `www` 301s to the apex; Google's canonical matches ours on every indexed page.
- `robots.txt` allows everything and points at the sitemap; `/llms.txt` is 200 (fixed since 10-09).
- Lighthouse SEO is 100 and accessibility 91-95 on every page tested.
